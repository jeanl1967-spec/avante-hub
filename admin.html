Discover locations from properties & activities

Scans every onboarded property, the full StockNetwork resort list, and all activities for the areas, towns and suburbs already on file, and adds any that aren't yet a Town or Suburb here — auto-suggesting a zone and coordinates from the source records. Geocode locations from coordinates

Automatically places every property, StockNetwork resort-list row, and activity that has a latitude/longitude but no location tag yet, using Google's Geocoding API — no manual entry needed. Never touches anything already tagged, whether that was set by hand or by an earlier run of this.

Re-check zones on everything already tagged

Re-sends every coordinate through Google again — including records already tagged — and corrects the zone wherever it comes back different (this is what fixed Garden Route towns like Knysna/Plettenberg Bay being tagged "Western Cape"; see the town-zone colours on the Master map below to check what's still wrong). Costs a fresh Google lookup for every coordinate, and can overwrite a zone you corrected by hand if Google or the keyword match disagrees with it — use this as a one-off cleanup, not routinely.

Improve property coordinates (from your StockNetwork export)

Most StockNetwork coordinates are rounded to about 1 km, so map pins can sit a kilometre out. Choose your StockNetwork resort export (the .csv you upload to the resort list) and this looks each property up by name on Google, then gives you two files: a review report (every property with a confidence rating and the suggested latitude/longitude), and a copy of your export with only the high-confidence coordinates swapped in, ready to load back into StockNetwork. It changes nothing in the hub itself. Each property looked up is one Google lookup.

How Payouts Work

Where the numbers come from, and how to get each affiliate paid

1

Add the affiliate here first

On the Affiliates tab, add each affiliate with their exact Hub affiliate ID (the same ID that's in their personal Hub link and their flight/booking links) and set what they earn for each channel — Accommodation, Flights, Activities, Car Hire, and Packages. These match the tabs in their own Hub. For each channel choose either a % of the revenue you log, or a fixed R flat amount paid every time you log an entry for that channel — whichever suits how you pay that affiliate.

2

Pull flight booking numbers from Impact.com

Log into app.impact.com, then in the top navigation go to Reports → More Reports → Performance by Sub ID and Shared ID (or Reports → Advanced Action Listing for a booking-by-booking view). Every affiliate's flight bookings are tagged with their Hub affiliate ID as the Sub ID, since the Hub's "Your Flight Link" appends it automatically. Filter or group by Sub ID to see each affiliate's revenue for the period.

3

Pull accommodation numbers from StockNetwork

Accommodation bookings run through each affiliate's own personal StockNetwork booking link rather than through Impact.com, so check StockNetwork's own reporting for booking revenue per affiliate over the same period.

4

Log the revenue on the Payouts tab

Select the affiliate, choose which channel the revenue came from (Accommodation, Flights, Activities, Car Hire, or Packages), enter a period label (e.g. "August 2026") and the total revenue for that period, then click Add Revenue Entry. The amount you owe them is calculated automatically using that channel's own revenue share percentage.

5

Pay them, then mark it paid

Once you've paid an affiliate (EFT, etc.), find that entry in their Payouts table and click Mark Paid. This updates their outstanding balance so you always know who's still owed money at a glance.

💡 If you enter more than one revenue entry for the same period by mistake, just delete the wrong one from the entries table — deleting an entry automatically reverses its effect on that affiliate's totals.
