How Payouts Work

Where the numbers come from, and how to get each affiliate paid

1

Add the affiliate here first

On the Affiliates tab, add each affiliate with their exact Hub affiliate ID (the same ID that's in their personal Hub link and their flight/booking links) and set their revenue share percentage — the cut they get of the bookings they drive.

2

Pull flight booking numbers from Impact.com

Log into app.impact.com, then in the top navigation go to Reports → More Reports → Performance by Sub ID and Shared ID (or Reports → Advanced Action Listing for a booking-by-booking view). Every affiliate's flight bookings are tagged with their Hub affiliate ID as the Sub ID, since the Hub's "Your Flight Link" appends it automatically. Filter or group by Sub ID to see each affiliate's revenue for the period.

3

Pull accommodation numbers from StockNetwork

Accommodation bookings run through each affiliate's own personal StockNetwork booking link rather than through Impact.com, so check StockNetwork's own reporting for booking revenue per affiliate over the same period.

4

Log the revenue on the Payouts tab

Select the affiliate, enter a period label (e.g. "August 2026") and the total revenue they generated in that period, then click Add Revenue Entry. The amount you owe them is calculated automatically using their current revenue share percentage.

5

Pay them, then mark it paid

Once you've paid an affiliate (EFT, etc.), find that entry in their Payouts table and click Mark Paid. This updates their outstanding balance so you always know who's still owed money at a glance.

💡 If you enter more than one revenue entry for the same period by mistake, just delete the wrong one from the entries table — deleting an entry automatically reverses its effect on that affiliate's totals.
